<objective>
Give the backend resource-scoped authorization: four predicates that answer "can this specific person act on this specific activity or organisation?", the decorators that apply them to routes, and a test that stops any future phase from shipping an activity- or org-scoped endpoint behind the coarse `organiser_required`.

Purpose: every authorization decision in Phases 2 through 8 — campaign push-live, Kanban tasks, QR check-in, org profile editing, the event channel, progression gating — resolves through these four functions. RBAC-02's promise is only real if it is mechanically enforced, so this plan ships the enforcement test alongside the predicates.

Output: `backend/app/rbac.py` extended with `can_manage_activity`, `can_participate_in_activity`, `can_manage_org`, `is_meetup_head`, the `activity_scoped` / `org_scoped` decorator factories, and two test files covering the predicate matrix and the route-guard gate.
